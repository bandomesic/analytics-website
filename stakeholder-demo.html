The product principle

Useful insight. Deliberate boundaries.

01

Run it on your infrastructure

The self-hosted foundation includes organizations, site management, authentication and installation snippets.

02

Collect less by design

Server-side sanitization, bounded event properties, rotating visitor estimates and approximate geography are implemented.

03

Keep access intentional

Organization membership is the access boundary. The designer uses short-lived authorization scoped to a site and origin.
